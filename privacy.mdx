Skye is a personal assistant in Telegram.

## What we receive

Messages, photos, voice notes, and files you send. Your Telegram user id. If you connect an app, that app sees what you allow.

## What we do with it

We use it to reply and to run the jobs you ask for. We do not sell it.

## Where it lives

If you run Skye on your own computer, it stays there. If you use a hosted instance, it is stored there so the service can run.

## Telegram

The chat also goes through Telegram, under [Telegram’s privacy policy](https://telegram.org/privacy).

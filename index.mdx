Skye is a personal assistant in Telegram. Write to her the way you write to a person: short, by voice, with a photo or a file. She picks up the context and helps you finish the task.

There is no extra app to install. If you prefer a browser, there is also a web chat at [chat.skye-bot.com](https://chat.skye-bot.com).

<Card title="Open Skye" icon="send" horizontal href="https://t.me/skye_ai_bot">
  Starts a chat in Telegram.
</Card>

## What she can do

- **Talk it through.** Ask a question, think out loud, or hand her a half-finished idea. She keeps the thread.
- **Listen and speak.** Send a voice note, or ask for an answer as a voice message.
- **See images.** Send a photo to describe, read, or edit. Or ask her to create a new picture.
- **Read documents.** PDFs, text, code, spreadsheets, and slides. Summaries, comparisons, key numbers.
- **Search the web.** Fresh facts with sources, plus YouTube transcripts.
- **Work with files and code.** A private workspace where she runs commands, writes files, and hands the result back.
- **Remember.** Small facts you want kept, private to you or shared with a group.
- **Learn jobs.** Upload a skill, connect an app, or build a custom agent.
- **Run on its own.** Scheduled and webhook automations that fire a task in the chat.

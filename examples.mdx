You do not need to phrase things carefully. Say what you want in your own words and Skye works out the rest. Below are real shapes of conversation. The prompts are short on purpose, and the transcripts are illustrative.

## A voice note becomes a plan

Send a rambling voice note; get back something you can keep.

  turns={[

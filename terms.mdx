These terms cover the hosted Skye bot in Telegram. If you run Skye on your own computer, you are responsible for that instance.

## Using Skye

You must be allowed to use Telegram. Do not use Skye to break the law, to harm people, or to send spam. Do not use it to attack systems, to scrape at scale, or to process content you have no right to process.

## Your content

You keep what you send. You let us process it so Skye can reply and use the tools you connect.

## Paid access

Some features are paid in Telegram Stars. Skye Plus is 449 Stars each month and renews every 30 days. Prices are shown in the bot. You can cancel renewal at any time; access continues to the end of the paid period. Refunds follow Telegram's Stars rules. For billing questions, use `/paysupport` in a private chat with Skye.

## Availability

Skye can be wrong, slow, or down. We provide the service as is, without warranty.

## Acceptable use

Do not use Skye to send unsolicited messages, to impersonate people, or to process data you are not allowed to handle. The instance owner may allow or ban access.

## Changes

We can change the service or these terms. The current text lives on this page.

Skye works in direct chats on the free plan. Skye Plus adds room for longer work and lets you build your own agents. Paid access uses **Telegram Stars**, Telegram's built-in payment method.

## Free

Everyday use, at no cost:

- a basic daily message allowance that resets each day;
- text, voice, images, documents, web search, and YouTube;
- skills, connectors, and projects;
- memory and automations.

When the daily allowance runs out, you can continue the next day. A monthly cap also applies.

## Skye Plus

- **449 Telegram Stars each month**, renewed every 30 days.
- An expanded daily and monthly message allowance, for longer and more frequent work.
- Create and edit your own custom agents.
- Cancel any time. You keep access to the end of the period you paid for.

Open `/account` in a private chat to see your plan and upgrade. Payment happens inside Telegram.

## New users

New accounts can receive a short Skye Plus preview, so you can try the paid features before deciding. The Account panel shows it while it is active.

## Managing your plan

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/account` | Your plan, upgrade, and cancel renewal. |
| `/paysupport` | Help with a Stars purchase. Opens a private chat with Skye. |
| `/terms` | The plan terms. |

Renewal is automatic on Skye Plus. Cancel it in `/account`: you keep access until the period ends and are not charged again. Refunds follow Telegram's Stars rules.

## Groups

A group is a shared space, so access works differently:

- A group works when it is on the instance's allowlist, or when one of its admins has Skye Plus or complimentary access.
- Group usage is billed to that paying admin, not to each speaker.
- Group settings, skills, and memory are shared; each forum topic has its own conversation.

If a group is not enabled, Skye stays silent there.

## Complimentary access

The instance owner can allow specific people or groups without payment. If you have complimentary access, `/account` will say so.

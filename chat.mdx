## What you can send

- **Text.** A question, a task, or a rough thought.
- **Voice.** A voice note is transcribed and answered like text. Ask for the reply as a voice message when you want one.
- **Photos and screenshots.** She can describe a picture, read text inside it, or edit it.
- **Documents.** PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, plain text, code, CSV, and more. Use the caption to say what you need.
- **Replies.** Reply to any message, including a photo or a document, to bring it into the task.
- **Links.** Paste a link for her to read or summarise.

You can put several files in one message, as an album, and give one instruction for all of them.

## What you get back

Skye answers in a few short messages rather than one long wall. Depending on the task she can also send a generated image, a voice message, or a file.

<Note>
  She decides which tools a request needs. You do not have to name a tool or a command.
</Note>

## Everyday controls

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/reset` | Starts a new conversation. Long-term memory is kept. |
| `/stop` | Cancels the task that is running. |
| `/catchup` | Summarises this conversation so far. |
| `/help` | Shows what she can do. |

See [Commands](/commands) for the full list.

## In a group

Add Skye to a group and talk normally. She stays quiet until she is called:

- mention her, reply to one of her messages, use a command, or write her name.

Groups and forum topics each keep their own conversation. Settings, skills, and group memory are shared across the group; each topic has its own history. Only messages that arrive after her last reply are used as context, so she does not repeat the room.

<Warning>
  In a group, anyone can see what is shared with the group. Keep private things in a direct chat.
</Warning>

If the web chat is enabled for your instance, open [chat.skye-bot.com](https://chat.skye-bot.com) and sign in with Telegram. It is a second way to talk to her, with the same account and the same projects. See [Projects](/projects).
